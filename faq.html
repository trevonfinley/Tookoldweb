Birmingham DJ FAQ

Answers before you send the inquiry.

These common questions help Birmingham and Alabama hosts understand what to share and what to expect when booking DJ Too Kold.

Booking questions

Plan with fewer unknowns.

What details should I send first?

Send your event date, city, venue, event type, guest count, start and end time, and the kind of music energy you want.

Can DJ Too Kold play clean music for school events?

Yes. Share clean-version requirements and any district or venue guidelines when you submit the booking inquiry.

Does DJ Too Kold serve events outside Birmingham?

Yes. Share the city, venue, and load-in details for Birmingham, nearby Central Alabama areas, or other Alabama event locations when requesting availability.

Do you support announcements and microphone moments?

Yes. Include entrances, speeches, games, raffles, special dances, or timeline moments that need microphone support.

Can I send must-play and do-not-play songs?

Yes. Must-play and do-not-play notes are part of the planning process so the set fits the host and the room.

What affects the final quote?

Event length, date, location, setup needs, travel, microphone needs, venue access, and custom requirements can all affect pricing.

Does the website process payments?

No. This site collects inquiries only. Booking confirmation, agreements, and payment steps happen after availability is reviewed.
